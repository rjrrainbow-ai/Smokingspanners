PRIVACY POLICY

Smoking Spanners Vehicle Repairs

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Your privacy matters.

Smoking Spanners Vehicle Repairs respects your privacy and is committed to handling customer information responsibly and in accordance with applicable UK data protection legislation.

1. Who We Are

Smoking Spanners Vehicle Repairs provides mobile vehicle repair, servicing and diagnostic services.

For privacy enquiries, please contact us on 07543 366465.

2. Information We Collect

When you contact us or request a quotation, we may receive:

  • Your name and telephone number.
  • Vehicle registration number.
  • Vehicle make and model.
  • Mileage, where provided.
  • Postcode or service location.
  • Vehicle faults or warning-light information.
  • Preferred appointment details.
  • Information included in WhatsApp, SMS or telephone enquiries.
  • Relevant service, invoice or payment records where applicable.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use customer information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and quotation requests.
  • Discuss vehicle faults and required repairs.
  • Arrange appointments.
  • Provide vehicle servicing and repair services.
  • Maintain relevant business and accounting records.
  • Comply with legal obligations.

Depending on the circumstances, processing may be necessary to take steps before entering a contract, perform a contract, meet legal obligations or pursue legitimate business interests.

4. Website Enquiry Forms

Our website allows customers to prepare enquiries using WhatsApp or SMS.

Information entered into the enquiry form is not stored in a customer database on this website.

When you select WhatsApp or SMS, a draft message is prepared. You decide whether to send it.

Messages that you send may subsequently be retained by Smoking Spanners Vehicle Repairs for legitimate customer service and business purposes.

5. Sharing Information

Customer information is not sold to advertisers.

Information may be processed by WhatsApp, mobile telephone networks, website hosting providers or other service providers involved in delivering our services.

Information may also be shared where necessary for legal compliance, professional advice or legitimate business operations.

6. Google and Facebook

Our website may contain links to Google and Facebook for customer reviews and business information.

When you open these websites, their own privacy policies and data processing practices apply.

We do not control the operation of these external platforms.

7. Website Hosting and Cookies

Our website is hosted using GitHub Pages.

The current website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies and does not use a customer account database.

GitHub may process technical information, including IP addresses, for security and hosting purposes.

Visit GitHub's Privacy Statement for further information.

8. How Long We Keep Information

Customer messages and relevant business records are retained only for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes for which they were collected, including servicing, accounting, legal obligations and handling disputes.

Records are reviewed and deleted or securely disposed of when no longer required.

9. Your Data Protection Rights

Subject to applicable UK data protection rules, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to your personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion where applicable.
  • Object to certain processing.
  • Request restriction of processing.
  • Request data portability where applicable.

To exercise your rights, contact Smoking Spanners Vehicle Repairs.

10. Complaints

If you are concerned about how your information has been handled, please contact us first.

You may also contact the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

Visit the ICO website

11. Updates to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services or information-handling practices change.

The latest version will be available on this page.
